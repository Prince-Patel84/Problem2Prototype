### Remediation & Flagged Items Section
Two stories (US-01 and US-05) were initially flagged as too large, potentially exceeding the 8-point threshold for a single sprint. To refine these stories for better sprint planning and execution, the following suggestions were implemented:
- **For US-01**: The story has been broken down into smaller, more specific tasks related to dynamic bundling, such as "Implement real-time rider compatibility validation" or "Develop the dynamic quote generation algorithm." This has made the story more manageable and estimable.
- **For US-05**: Similarly, the story has been refined to focus on smaller tasks focusing on specific aspects of actuarial pricing and underwriting STP, such as "Implement multi-rider discount algorithms" or "Develop the automated underwriting STP process." This has helped in better sprint planning and execution.

For both stories, the acceptance criteria have been further refined to include concrete, automatable tests for each sub-task, making the stories more testable and ensuring that they meet the INVEST criteria.
